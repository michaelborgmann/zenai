title = 'AI Weather Forecasts on PC'

A British research group has developed an AI system capable of generating complete [weather forecasts](https://www.cam.ac.uk/research/news/fully-ai-driven-weather-prediction-system-could-start-revolution-in-forecasting) using just a standard desktop PC. Traditionally, weather predictions required powerful supercomputers to process large datasets, but this new model can create accurate global and regional forecasts by gathering data from satellites and weather stations. This advancement makes weather forecasting more resource-efficient and faster. The key benefit is that even financially limited regions, particularly in the Global South, can now access competitive weather prediction capabilities, democratizing access to weather forecasting technology.
