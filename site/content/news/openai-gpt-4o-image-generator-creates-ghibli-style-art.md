title = 'OpenAI’s GPT-4o Image Generator Creates Ghibli Style Art'

OpenAI has released a new image generator based on its [GPT-4o](https://openai.com/index/hello-gpt-4o/) model, which has quickly gone viral for turning users' photos into images in the iconic style of Studio Ghibli films like My Neighbor Totoro and Castle in the Sky. The tool allows easy uploads in ChatGPT to create these animations. While popular, the feature raises potential copyright issues, as the model likely trained on images from Ghibli films. This could lead to legal challenges, similar to ongoing lawsuits against other AI platforms.
