title = 'Google Expands Veo 3 AI Video Model'

Google has expanded its AI video model [Veo 3](https://deepmind.google/models/veo/) to over 70 countries shortly after launch, excluding the EU for now. Josh Woodward, VP of Gemini at Google, announced the rollout on platform X. Veo 3 quickly went viral with impressive social media demos. Access is tiered by subscription: Gemini Pro users get a one-time 10-generation trial, while Premium Ultra subscribers pay $250/month for maximum daily usage. The model excels in gaming visuals but costs around $0.75 per second of video. Its capabilities raise concerns about generative AI’s role in spreading misinformation and deepfakes.
