[Midjourney(https://www.midjourney.com) has launched an alpha version of its [V7](https://www.midjourney.com/updates/v7-alpha) image generation model, offering improved quality and personalization. Users can rate 200 image pairs to customize the model, while the new "Draft Mode" speeds up image creation and reduces costs by half, with voice input support.
