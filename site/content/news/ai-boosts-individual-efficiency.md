title = 'AI Boosts Individual Efficiency'

The [Cybernetic Teammate](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5188231) study by Procter & Gamble with 776 professionals revealed that individuals using AI support can achieve results as good as traditional two-person teams. However, AI-supported teams outperformed both individuals and traditional teams, producing better and faster results. AI also helped reduce performance gaps between technical and non-technical experts, allowing even less experienced participants to contribute effectively. The study, conducted in one-day workshops, may not fully reflect long-term, complex workplace dynamics.
