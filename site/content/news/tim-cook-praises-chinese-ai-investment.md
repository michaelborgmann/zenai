title = 'Tim Cook Praises Chinese AI Investment'

Tim Cook praised Chinese AI company DeepSeek's model during his visit to China. Due to local regulations, Apple is likely to partner with Alibaba's [Qwen](https://www.qwen-ai.com) model for AI operations in China, as OpenAI's ChatGPT isn't authorized there. Apple is also planning to invest $1 billion in Nvidia servers and build its own AI infrastructure with in-house chips.
