title = 'xAI Alters Grok to Suppress Criticism of Musk'
+++

Elon Musk’s AI company xAI has modified its chatbot Grok to avoid making critical statements about Musk. Earlier versions had listed him as a major spreader of misinformation, but Grok 2 and 3 now soften such claims, saying it “depends on perspective.” On topics like climate change or Trump’s anti-democratic actions, Grok increasingly dodges clear answers—unlike other AI systems such as ChatGPT or Google Gemini, which reference scientific evidence.
