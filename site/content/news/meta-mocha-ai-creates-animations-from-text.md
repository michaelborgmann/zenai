title = 'Meta’’s MoCha AI Creates Animations from Text'
+++

Meta, in collaboration with the University of Waterloo, has introduced [MoCha](https://congwei1230.github.io/MoCha/), an AI system that generates full character animations from text and speech. Unlike previous models, MoCha creates full-body movements, synchronized lip movements, natural gestures, and interactions between multiple characters. The 30-billion-parameter model produces HD videos of about five seconds and solves lip-syncing issues with a unique mechanism. In tests with 150 scenarios, MoCha outperformed similar models.
