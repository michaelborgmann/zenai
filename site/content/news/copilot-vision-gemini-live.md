title = 'Copilot Vision in Edge, Gemini Live for Android'
+++

Microsoft has rolled out its visual AI assistant, [Copilot Vision](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/edge/copilot/vision), to the Edge browser. The feature allows users to interact with web content through natural language, though it’s currently limited to select sites like Wikipedia, OpenTable, and TripAdvisor. Microsoft plans to extend the feature to Windows in the future. Meanwhile, Google has made its visual assistant [Gemini Live](https://gemini.google/overview/gemini-live/) available for free on all Android devices. Gemini Live enables users to share screen content or activate the phone’s camera for real-time, image-based interactions with the AI.
