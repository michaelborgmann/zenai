title = 'MIT Study on Emotional Impact of ChatGPT'
+++

A joint study by OpenAI and MIT Media Lab examined the [emotional impacts of ChatGPT](https://www.media.mit.edu/posts/openai-mit-research-collaboration-affective-use-and-emotional-wellbeing-in-ChatGPT/) and its Advanced Voice Mode. It found mixed reactions, with heavy users reporting more dependency and worsened moods. Short interactions with the Voice Mode improved well-being, while longer use had negative effects. The study highlighted the complexity of AI's influence on mental health and raised concerns about potential misuse of emotional data.
