title = 'eBay Uses Personal Data for AI'
+++

eBay has updated its privacy policy to allow the use of personal data for training its AI models, aiming to enhance its services and develop new features. This includes the use of personal information such as names, email addresses, ID data, and social security numbers. However, eBay assures responsible AI practices and offers users the option to opt out of data usage via account settings.
