Adobe has introduced an update for Premiere Pro with several new [AI features](https://news.adobe.com/news/2025/04/new-ai-innovation-in-industry). The key addition is Generative Extend, now fully available and supporting 4K footage. This tool extends clips or continues shortened ones, with AI-generated sections marked on the timeline. Other new features include Adobe Media Intelligence, which analyzes video content and enables natural language searches, and enhanced automatic subtitles that can now be translated into up to 27 languages. The update is available to Creative Cloud customers.
