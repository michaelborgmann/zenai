title = 'Meta’s $1000 Ray-Ban Smart Glasses with Display'

Meta is developing a new version of its Ray-Ban smart glasses, codenamed "Hypernova," which will feature an integrated display at the bottom of the lenses. The price is expected to exceed $1,000. Current models already offer features like vision-sharing and communication through headphones and Meta AI, but these AI functions are limited in the EU due to data privacy regulations. Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg views smart glasses as a forward-looking device category, one that is also being actively explored by companies like OpenAI and Google.
