title = 'Nvidia Builds Cheaper Blackwell GPU for China'
+++

Nvidia plans to produce a simplified, more affordable version of its [Blackwell GPU](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/technologies/blackwell-architecture/) for the Chinese market starting in June. Based on the [RTX Pro 6000D](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/products/workstations/professional-desktop-gpus/rtx-pro-6000/) and priced between $6,500 and $8,000, this new chip uses conventional memory instead of advanced TSMC technology. The move responds to U.S. export restrictions limiting China’s access to high-performance GPUs.
