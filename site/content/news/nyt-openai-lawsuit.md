A U.S. judge has allowed The New York Times' lawsuit against OpenAI to proceed. The newspaper accuses OpenAI of unauthorized use of its articles for AI training and reproducing content in ChatGPT responses. OpenAI defends itself under fair use, arguing its AI benefits the public and research. The case could have major implications for AI firms and publishers concerned about traffic loss due to AI-generated summaries.
