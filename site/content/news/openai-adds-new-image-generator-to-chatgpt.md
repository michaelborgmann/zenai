title = 'OpenAI Adds New Image Generator to ChatGPT'
+++

OpenAI has enhanced ChatGPT with a new, more powerful [image generator](https://openai.com/index/introducing-4o-image-generation/), replacing the previous DALL-E model. The new generator is integrated directly into the Omnimodel GPT-4o, allowing it to process text, audio, and images simultaneously. This results in improved image quality, including more precise text representation, transparent backgrounds, and better visualization of diagrams and logos.
