title = 'ByteDance Launches InfiniteYou AI for Portraits'

[ByteDance](https://www.bytedance.com) has introduced [InfiniteYou](https://github.com/bytedance/InfiniteYou), an AI system designed to improve portrait photo generation. It uses a new approach with a component called InfuseNet to analyze and extract facial features, allowing for more accurate image creation. Unlike previous systems, ByteDance processes these features as an additional layer of information, enhancing likeness and adherence to text instructions. While the system offers faster image creation, developers acknowledge the need for improvements in facial similarity and image quality and caution against potential misuse for creating fake photos.
