title = 'Alibaba Qwen2.5-VL-32B: New Advanced Image Analysis'

Alibaba released [Qwen2.5-VL-32B](https://qwenlm.github.io/blog/qwen2.5-vl-32b/), an updated multimodal AI model with 32 billion parameters, surpassing similar open models like Gemma 3-27B and Mistral Small 3.1 24B in initial benchmarks. It performs especially well in image analysis, providing detailed and accurate interpretations of complex visuals. The development team plans to focus on enhancing reasoning processes to tackle even more complex visual tasks. Alibaba previously introduced its first multimodal model with reasoning capabilities, QVQ, in late 2024.
