Anthropic has introduced a [new premium subscription](https://www.anthropic.com/pricing) for its Claude model called the "Max Plan." Targeted at heavy users, it offers significantly higher usage limits and priority access to new features. Available in two pricing tiers—$100 and $200 per month—it provides 5x and 20x more usage resources than the previous Pro plan. The Max Plan is available globally in all Claude regions, mirroring a similar offering from OpenAI for its GPT-4o model.
