title = 'Krisp Filters Accents in Real Time'

[Krisp](https://krisp.ai), a meeting assistant, has added a new feature to its AI-powered desktop app that can filter out accents in real-time during conversations. The initial beta converts Indian accents into standard English, with plans to expand to other accents, including Filipino, Latin American, and European varieties. Krisp integrates with popular conferencing platforms like Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet. Unlike other AI assistants, Krisp operates in the background on the device, processing speech with a latency of just 200 milliseconds.
