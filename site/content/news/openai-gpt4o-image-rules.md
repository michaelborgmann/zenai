OpenAI has adopted a more liberal approach to [image generation in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/policies/creating-images-and-videos-in-line-with-our-policies/), focusing on precise safeguards rather than blanket bans. The goal is to give users more creative freedom while still maintaining control over harmful content. Hate symbols, like swastikas, are no longer automatically blocked if used in educational or cultural contexts. Public figures can opt out of being depicted, and stricter protections are enforced for images involving minors.
