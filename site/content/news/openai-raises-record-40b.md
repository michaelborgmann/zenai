OpenAI has raised a record-breaking $40 billion, with SoftBank contributing $30 billion under the condition that OpenAI fully transitions to a for-profit model by year-end. This funding pushes OpenAI’s valuation to $300 billion, making it one of the most valuable private tech companies, alongside ByteDance and just behind SpaceX.
