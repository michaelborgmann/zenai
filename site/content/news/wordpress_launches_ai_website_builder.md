title = 'WordPress Launches AI Website Builder'

WordPress has introduced a free [AI-powered website builder](https://wordpress.com/ai-website-builder/?ref=blog) aimed at small businesses, bloggers, and creators—though hosting may incur additional costs. While not suited for e-commerce, the tool simplifies site creation through a prompt-based setup, customizable design options, and a built-in AI assistant for guidance. Users can easily adjust layouts, colors, and content, enabling attractive websites without technical expertise.
