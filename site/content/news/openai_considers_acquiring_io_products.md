OpenAI is reportedly interested in acquiring io Products, a stealth startup from former Apple design chief [Jony Ive’s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jony_Ive) company, LoveFrom. The startup is developing a screenless AI device, with OpenAI potentially investing over $500 million. CEO Sam Altman is already involved, and the goal seems to be creating a portable ChatGPT voice assistant, akin to the AI assistant in Her.
