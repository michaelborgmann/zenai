title = 'Google Launches NotebookLM AI App'
+++

Google’s AI research tool, [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com), currently available only through the browser, will soon launch as a mobile app for Android and iOS. While the web interface suits it well, a native desktop version isn’t expected. The mobile app will improve discoverability in app stores and address issues with the smartphone version of the web tool.
