[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com) has improved its AI assistant Claude's problem-solving abilities with a simple "think" command. This method adds a [thinking](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/extended-thinking) step, allowing Claude to record its thoughts in a separate space before generating a response. Tests showed impressive results, including a 54% improvement in customer service scenarios.
