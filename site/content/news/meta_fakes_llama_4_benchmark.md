title = 'Meta Fakes Llama 4 Benchmark'
+++

Meta allegedly inflated the benchmark results for its Llama 4 models. In a [blog post](https://ai.meta.com/blog/llama-4-multimodal-intelligence/), they claimed an ELO score of 1417, surpassing GPT-4 and just below Google’s Gemini 2.5 Pro. However, testers noticed the version used, Llama 4 Maverick optimized for conversationality, differed from the publicly available model. In response, [LMArena](https://lmarena.ai) has updated its rules to ensure fairer and more reproducible results.
