title = 'Better RAG Performance with Fewer Documents'
+++

A [study from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://github.com/shaharl6000/MoreDocsSameLen) found that [RAG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retrieval-augmented_generation) models perform better with fewer, but highly relevant documents. Tests with [Llama-3.1](https://www.llama.com/llama3_1/) and [Gemma 2](https://ai.google.dev/gemma) showed up to 10% improvement, as irrelevant yet similar documents can confuse AI models and degrade accuracy.
