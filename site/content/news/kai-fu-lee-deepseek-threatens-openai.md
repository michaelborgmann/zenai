[Kai-Fu Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai-Fu_Lee), former head of [Google China
(https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_China), views [DeepSeek](https://www.deepseek.com), a Chinese AI startup offering free open-source models, as an existential threat to OpenAI. Lee's company, [01.AI](https://www.01.ai), now fully relies on Deepseek models, as they have sparked a "ChatGPT moment" in China. Many users have canceled their paid ChatGPT subscriptions due to Deepseek's free offerings. Lee highlights that Deepseek's cost efficiency, running on just 2% of OpenAI's expenses, raises concerns about the sustainability of OpenAI's more expensive model. Lee predicts 01.AI's revenue will reach $13 million in early 2025. Despite growth, the company is not yet profitable.
