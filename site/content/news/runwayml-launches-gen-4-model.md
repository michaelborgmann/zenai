[RunwayML](https://runwayml.com) has unveiled [Gen-4](https://runwayml.com/research/introducing-runway-gen-4), a new AI model for video generation that improves dynamic video creation and consistency in styles and objects. A key feature is its ability to generate consistent characters across different lighting, locations, and scenes using just one reference image. The model is now available to all paying subscribers and enterprise customers, with reference functions coming soon.
