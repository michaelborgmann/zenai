[xAI](https://x.ai) has added visual capabilities to its chatbot Grok through a new feature called Grok Vision. Available first on iPhones, the update allows users to point their phone at objects, signs, or documents and receive contextual explanations, while also enabling screen content analysis. Though Android support is on the way, new search tools and voice modes are already live on that platform for paying users. Grok can now respond in multiple languages.
