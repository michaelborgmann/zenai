Amazon is testing a new AI feature, "[Buy for me](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-shopping-app-buy-for-me-brands)," that allows customers to order products from external online stores. Using Nova and Claude AI, the agent selects items and securely enters payment and delivery details. This feature is likely powered by the new Nova Act, which can perform tasks on external websites.
