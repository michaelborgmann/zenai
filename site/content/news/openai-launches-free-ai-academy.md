title = 'OpenAI Launches Free AI Academy'

OpenAI has launched the [OpenAI Academy](https://academy.openai.com), a free platform offering dozens of hours of video content and events aimed at providing accessible AI education. The academy includes courses like "ChatGPT on Campus," "ChatGPT at Work," "Sora Tutorials," "AI for K-12 Educators," and "Prompt Engineering." Currently available in English, the platform plans to expand to other languages soon.
