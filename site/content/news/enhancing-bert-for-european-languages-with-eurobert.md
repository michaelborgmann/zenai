[EuroBERT](https://huggingface.co/EuroBERT) is a multilingual encoder model developed by a research-industry consortium to improve performance in European languages. Built on the [BERT](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04805) architecture, it excels in German text tasks, offering faster processing and better handling of long texts. Unlike many models trained primarily on English, EuroBERT adapts easily to other languages, solving the issue of suboptimal fine-tuning for German.
