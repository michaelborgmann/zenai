title = 'OpenAI Adopts Competitor Anthropics Open Model Standard'

OpenAI has integrated the [Model Context Protocol](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-context-protocol) (MCP) from competitor Anthropic into its products, including the ChatGPT desktop app. MCP standardizes how AI applications access data, allowing developers to connect to various data sources without building unique interfaces for each. With growing adoption from companies like Block and Replit, MCP could become a key standard in AI, fostering a more open ecosystem.
