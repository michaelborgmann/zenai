title = 'Meta Launches Llama-4 Models Scout & Maverick'
+++

Meta has launched the first two [Llama-4](https://ai.meta.com/blog/llama-4-multimodal-intelligence/) models, Scout and Maverick, designed to process both text and images. Trained with the Behemoth model, they feature 109 billion and 400 billion parameters, respectively. Both can run on a single H100 GPU. Meta claims these models are more politically neutral than earlier versions.
