title = 'Amazon Launches Nova Sonic Voice Model'

Amazon's new [Nova Sonic](https://aws.amazon.com/ai/generative-ai/nova/speech/) voice model rivals OpenAI and Google in speech quality and speed, while costing around 80% less than GPT-4o’s voice mode. It’s available via [Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock) and already powers Alexa+, offering robust recognition even in noisy settings.
