title = 'OpenAI Pushes for EU AI Strategy Overhaul'
+++

In its Economic Blueprint, OpenAI calls for a major reset of the EU’s AI strategy, centered on four principles: better infrastructure, reduced bureaucracy, deeper AI integration in daily life, and alignment with European values. The plan includes ambitious goals, like tripling AI compute capacity by 2030 and creating a renewable-powered "Green AI Grid." It also proposes a €1B AI Accelerator Fund and an AI Readiness Index to drive progress.
