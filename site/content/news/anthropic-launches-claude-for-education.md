title = 'Anthropic Launches Claude for Education'

AI company Anthropic is entering the education market with a tailored offering for universities. "Claude for Education" features a specialized learning mode that encourages students to think critically through targeted questions, rather than providing direct answers. Northeastern University, the London School of Economics, and Champlain College are already using Claude across their campuses.
