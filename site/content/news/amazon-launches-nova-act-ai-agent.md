title = 'Amazon Launches Nova Act AI Agent'
+++

Amazon has introduced [Nova Act](https://labs.amazon.science/blog/nova-act), an AI agent similar to OpenAI's [Operator](https://openai.com/index/introducing-operator/), capable of autonomously performing tasks in web browsers. U.S. customers and developers can preview the SDK at nova.amazon.com. The tool breaks down complex workflows into manageable commands for tasks like web searches, payment processing, and answering questions.
