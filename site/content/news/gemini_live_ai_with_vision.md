[Google’s Gemini Live](https://gemini.google/overview/gemini-live) lets Android users share their camera and screen with the AI, enabling smarter help with things like tech support or outfit ideas. It works in 45 languages and is free on newer phones like the Pixel 9, while older devices need a paid plan.
