The focus of criticism isn't necessarily the laws themselves, like the AI Act or GDPR, but rather the slow and complex regulatory processes involved. While the AI Act addresses general-purpose AI products, GDPR impacts the training of AI models with data, such as ensuring German-language models are trained on local data. The issue, according to Renz, lies in slow processes and a lack of innovation-friendly regulation. A more solutions-oriented approach from data protection authorities would help, as clarity on what’s allowed is essential for companies looking to bring their products to market.
