Welcome to ZenAI, a journey in demystifying artificial intelligence from a developer’s perspective. This project serves as a structured exploration of AI concepts, practical applications, and personal insights gained along the way.

## Why ZenAI?

As developers, we constantly seek to expand our understanding of emerging technologies. AI and machine learning are no longer just niche fields—they are transforming the way we build applications. ZenAI is a platform for learning, experimenting, and sharing insights about AI, machine learning, and deep learning.

## What You’ll Find Here

* **Blog Posts & Articles** – In-depth explorations of AI topics, hands-on tutorials, and industry trends.
* **Book & Article Summaries** – Condensed insights from essential AI and ML literature.
* **Code & Experiments** – Practical examples, projects, and proofs-of-concept to reinforce AI concepts.
* **Resources & References** – Curated lists of tools, libraries, and learning materials.

## About the Author

This project is maintained by [Michael Borgmann](https://www.michaelborgmann.com), a developer passionate about artificial intelligence and its real-world applications. My goal is to document my learning journey while making AI more accessible to other developers.

## Join the Journey

AI is a rapidly evolving field, and there’s always something new to explore. Follow along, contribute, or share your insights—let’s grow together in mastering AI!
